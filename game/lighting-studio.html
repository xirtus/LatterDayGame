How to use the studio

Staging guide

1 · Start from a place

Choose a chapter in the library or the Place & time panel. The studio loads that chapter's real geometry, then applies its research-informed lighting preset.

2 · Set the moment

Change the date and clock. With “Sun follows date & clock” on, sun azimuth/elevation and moon phase recompute from the chapter coordinates.

3 · Layer a mood

Moods, weather, seasons and times are partial layers. Apply them in any order; they tint the current place without resetting it.

4 · Shape the image

Use the key/fill/rim rig, practicals, fog, grade and post-processing. Vignette, grain, bloom, chromatic aberration and letterbox are all controllable.

5 · Stage the crowd

Crowd & props adds simple blocking figures, firearms, pitchforks, torches and muzzle flashes. The historical Carthage preset has zero torches and zero pitchforks; the stylised nightmare preset has both.

6 · Save and hand off

Save a look in this browser, copy a share link, or export JSON. “Copy game config” exports a reviewable look for the shared chapter lighting runtime.

Keyboard: H hide/show UI · R reset to current chapter preset · Space play/pause time. The research report at docs/weather-lighting-report.html explains every chapter choice.
